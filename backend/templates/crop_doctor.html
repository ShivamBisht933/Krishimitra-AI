About Anthracnose

Anthracnose is a fungal disease that can affect Black Gram leaves, stems and pods.

Common Symptoms

  • Dark brown or black spots on leaves.
  • Sunken lesions on infected plant parts.
  • In severe cases, lesions may spread to stems and pods.

Preventive Measures

  • Use healthy, disease-free seeds.
  • Remove infected plant residues from the field.
  • Avoid excessive moisture and overcrowding.
  • Monitor plants regularly for new symptoms.

{% elif result.predicted_disease == "Leaf Crinkle" %}

About Leaf Crinkle

Leaf Crinkle is a viral disease associated with abnormal leaf development in Black Gram.

Common Symptoms

  • Crinkling and puckering of leaves.
  • Leaf curling and deformation.
  • Reduced plant growth in affected plants.

Preventive Measures

  • Use healthy and certified seeds.
  • Monitor the field for abnormal leaf growth.
  • Remove severely affected plants where appropriate.
  • Monitor possible insect vectors and seek local agricultural advice.
{% elif result.predicted_disease == "Powdery Mildew" %}

About Powdery Mildew

Powdery Mildew is a fungal disease commonly recognized by powder-like fungal growth on plant surfaces.

Common Symptoms

  • White powdery patches on leaves.
  • Gradual yellowing of affected leaves.
  • In severe cases, drying of leaves.

Preventive Measures

  • Maintain proper spacing between plants.
  • Monitor leaves regularly for white patches.
  • Remove heavily infected plant residues.
  • Avoid unnecessary excessive nitrogen application.
{% elif result.predicted_disease == "Yellow Mosaic" %}

About Yellow Mosaic

Yellow Mosaic is a viral disease of Black Gram, commonly associated with whitefly transmission.

Common Symptoms

  • Yellow and green mosaic-like patterns on leaves.
  • Yellowing of affected leaf areas.
  • Reduced plant growth and yield in severe cases.

Preventive Measures

  • Use resistant varieties recommended for your region.
  • Monitor crops for whitefly activity.
  • Remove severely affected plants where appropriate.
  • Maintain field sanitation and control weeds that may host vectors.
